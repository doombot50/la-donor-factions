Louisiana's Vendor Factions

Donors show who funds a campaign. Vendors show who runs it. Every Louisiana committee reports the consultants, pollsters, printers and media buyers it pays, and two committees paying the same operative are, in practice, part of the same operation. This page reads those payments both ways: which firms work for whom, and which committees share a firm.

Method & sources

What counts as an operative

Built by build_vendor_factions.py from every Louisiana Ethics Administration expenditure filing since 2000 (2008 is missing from the upstream release). A payee carries no ID, so spellings are folded together (“Littlefield Consulting”, “Littlefield Consultants”) and a handful of known renames are merged.

The hard part is that most payees are not operatives. Each one is given a type, first from its name and then from what committees said they paid it for, summed by dollars across every filing. Only operatives and field workers can link two committees.

Three choices worth knowing

Stations are left out of the links. Two committees that bought time on WAFB are not allied; they are both running in Baton Rouge. That money is mapped on Louisiana Airwaves and Louisiana Dial; here each committee just carries its media total.

Private individuals are never named. Canvassers, election-day workers and drivers are real evidence of a shared field operation, so they count toward a link, but they are not public figures and appear only as a count. People paid as named professionals (fundraisers, consultants) are listed like any firm. Former candidates paid as people are treated as contributions, since that is almost always what those rows are.

Very common firms still count in the table, not the graph. A printer used by 600 committees tells you nothing about who is allied, so the graph drops any vendor paid by 100 or more committees. The firm table keeps them, because “who prints for whom” is still worth knowing.

Cycles and party

A cycle is the four years ending in a governor's election: the 2023 cycle runs 2020–2023, and 2027 is the cycle now under way. Party is the filer's own label, corrected where the filing says OTH but the alignment is public record (Gumbo PAC is Democratic, the RGA's Right Direction PAC Republican), from the same list Airwaves and the finance dashboard use.
